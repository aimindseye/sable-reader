# Sable Reader 1.0.1

A keyboard-first reader for BlackBerry-style Android phones such as the Zinwa Q25 and the Unihertz Titan 2.

- Opens EPUB books, comics (CBZ, CBR), audiobooks (M4B) and PDF.
- Driven from the physical keys and the trackpad as well as by touch; phones without arrow keys move with I J K L.
- Device profiles for the Q25, Q27, Titan 2, Titan 2 Elite and P26.
- Follow phone, Light and Dark themes with accents, and a first-run setup wizard.
- Screen-reader support: switch, checkbox and radio roles and labelled arrow controls.
- Offline: no internet, no camera, no account.

Package name: `org.sableos.reader`. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
